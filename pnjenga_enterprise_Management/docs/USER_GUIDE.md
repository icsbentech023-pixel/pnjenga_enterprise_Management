# User Guide

## For staff (data entry)

Open the data entry link on your phone. Enter your PIN. Choose the **Business** every time (Gathaiti or Kanjuku).

### Daily sales
1. Pick the date, business, category and item. Unit cost and price fill in automatically.
2. Enter the quantity. Total and profit are shown.
3. Tap **Save entry**. The new row appears under *Recent entries*.

Enter each sale line separately (one item and quantity per entry). If the price differs from the list, change the **Unit price** before saving.


**Low stock warning:** after you save a sale, a message in amber such as *Low stock: 6kg cylinder (full) (5 left, reorder at 5)* means it is time to reorder that item. Tell the owner.

### Stock received
Record each delivery when it arrives: business, item, quantity, unit cost and supplier.

### Expenses
Pick the type (transport, wages, rent, electricity, other), the amount and a short note.

### M-Pesa (once a day, at closing)
Open the **M-Pesa** tab. Choose the business and date, then enter:
1. **Opening float and cash.** They are filled in from the last closing. Check that they match what you started the day with.
2. **Total deposits and withdrawals** handled today.
3. **Commission earned** today.
4. **Owner top-up (+) or withdrawal (−)** if the owner added or took out money (leave blank if none).
5. **Closing float and cash**, counted at closing.

The form shows the **Variance**: (closing float + cash) − (opening float + cash) − commission − top-up. It should be **0**. If it is not, recount the cash and float, and check the M-Pesa statement. If you save with a variance, the form asks you to confirm.

Only one M-Pesa record is allowed per business per day. If you made a mistake, delete it and enter it again.

### Items (owner)
The **Items** tab is the price list. It asks for the owner PIN.
- **Add an item:** choose Gas or Agrovet, type the item name, the unit cost, the unit price and an optional reorder level, then tap **Save item**.
- **Change prices or the reorder level:** tap **Edit** beside the item, change the numbers and tap **Update item**. The name and category cannot be changed, because past sales and stock are matched by name. To rename an item, add the new one and delete the old one.
- **Delete** removes the item from the price list only. Past entries stay.
- New prices apply to new entries only. Old entries keep the price they were saved with.

### Mistakes
Use **Delete** on a recent entry and enter it again correctly. Only the 40 most recent entries per tab are shown.

## For the owner (dashboard)

Open the dashboard link and enter the owner PIN.

- **Filters:** a period (Today, Yesterday, 7 days, 30 days, This month, All time) or your own From and To dates, then the business (Both, Gathaiti, Kanjuku) and the category (All, Gas, M-Pesa, Agrovet)
- **Top tiles:** revenue, gross profit, expenses, net profit and average net profit per day for the selected range, each with the change against the previous period of the same length
- **Daily chart:** one bar per day, split by business, with a trend line. Switch between Revenue, Gross profit and Net profit. Best and slowest days are shown under the chart
- **Today:** today's revenue and gross profit per business, with yesterday's gross profit for comparison
- **Profit by business:** combined net profit split between Gathaiti (amber) and Kanjuku (blue), with gross profit by category
- **M-Pesa float & cash:** last closing float and cash, deposits, withdrawals, commission and variance for the range. Red shows how many days did not reconcile
- **Day by day:** a table of the latest 31 days in the range with revenue, gross profit, expenses and net profit
- **Reorder alerts:** a card listing every item that is LOW or OUT, with a red count in the top bar. Set the levels in the Items tab (Reorder level column)
- **Stock:** received minus sold for each gas size and agrovet item, with the reorder level and a status (OK, LOW, OUT)
- **Refresh** (blue) reloads the data. **Download PDF** (gold) prints the current view

Expenses are entered per business without a category, so they appear in totals only when the category filter is set to *All categories*.
